Interstates, rail and the airport

The report says Salt Lake City intersects Interstate 80, Interstate 15 and the Union Pacific Railroad Intermodal Hub, has 135 miles of commuter and light rail, and that downtown is just ten minutes from Salt Lake International Airport.

Source: Salt Lake City Department of Economic Development, Doing Business in Salt Lake City 2025 · As of: 2025 edition (posted June 2025); read October 21, 2026

Zoning & Land Use in Salt Lake City

Salt Lake City’s zoning rules are the Zoning Ordinance, Title 21A of the city code. The Planning Division’s Zoning page says to use the online zoning map to find the zoning for any property in the city, and to check the Land Use Tables in Chapter 21A.33 to see what is allowed in that zone. It also says the Planning Division can only provide information on properties within Salt Lake City’s jurisdictional boundaries, so confirm the property is inside the city first.

Salt Lake City Commercial Real Estate FAQ

What are office and industrial vacancy rates in Salt Lake City?

The city’s Doing Business in Salt Lake City 2025 report, citing CoStar, lists a 10.7% office vacancy rate and a 7% industrial vacancy rate, with average market rents of $26.13 and $10.87 per sq.ft. (Source: Salt Lake City Department of Economic Development, Doing Business in Salt Lake City 2025, as of 2025 edition)

Who are the largest employers in Salt Lake City?

The 2025 report lists Intermountain Health and The University of Utah at 15,000–20,000 employees each, Delta at 4,000–4,999, and Amazon, ARUP Laboratories and UPS at 3,000–3,999 each. (Source: Salt Lake City Department of Economic Development, Doing Business in Salt Lake City 2025, as of 2025 edition)

How do I find the zoning for a Salt Lake City property?

Look the property up on the city’s online zoning map, then check the Land Use Tables in Chapter 21A.33 of the Zoning Ordinance (Title 21A) for what that zone allows. (Source: Salt Lake City Planning Division, Zoning, as of October 21, 2026)

Will Salt Lake City planners answer questions about any Salt Lake City address?

Only for properties inside Salt Lake City’s jurisdictional boundaries, according to the city’s Zoning page, so confirm the property is in the city before you ask. (Source: Salt Lake City Planning Division, Zoning, as of October 21, 2026)

How far is downtown Salt Lake City from the airport?

Just ten minutes from Salt Lake International Airport, according to the city’s 2025 Doing Business report. (Source: Salt Lake City Department of Economic Development, Doing Business in Salt Lake City 2025, as of 2025 edition)
