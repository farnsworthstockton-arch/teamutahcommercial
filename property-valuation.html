Sources

Ranges show the middle half (25th to 75th percentile) of county-assessed market value per building square foot for commercial parcels in each county. Source: county assessor tax rolls published by the State of Utah, SGID (Utah Geospatial Resource Center), Parcels LIR, licensed CC BY 4.0. 2026 tax year for Salt Lake, Utah, Weber, Washington and Cache; 2025 for Davis and Tooele. Utah assesses property at fair market value as of January 1 (Utah Code 59-2-103), and a single building's assessed value can differ from its sale price. Not an appraisal.

  • Value per square foot is each parcel's total assessed market value (land and buildings together) divided by its building square feet. Tax-exempt parcels and buildings under 500 SF are left out, and the top and bottom 1% are trimmed.
  • Property type. Salt Lake County's roll splits office, retail and industrial; hospitality uses its general “Commercial” class. The other counties' rolls don't split commercial by type, so every type uses that county's all-commercial range.
  • Land is assessed value per acre of vacant commercial parcels (0.25 acre or more) in Salt Lake, Utah, Weber and Tooele counties. The other counties' rolls don't separate commercial land from house lots.
  • Age adjustment is the typical ratio of a parcel's value per square foot to its county median, by building age (2026 minus year built), pooled across the seven counties: 0–5 years 1.272, 6–15 years 1.249, 16–30 years 1.075, 31–50 years 0.86, 51+ years 0.885.
  • Other counties (for example Summit or Box Elder) use the seven counties above pooled together. Summit County's roll has no building square footage, and Box Elder's published roll is from 2020.
