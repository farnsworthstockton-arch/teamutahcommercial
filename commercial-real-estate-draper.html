Draper Commercial Real Estate Basics

Population

50,652

residents, the U.S. Census Bureau estimate for Draper on July 1, 2025. The April 1, 2020 estimates base was 51,026.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, City and Town Population Totals: 2020-2025 · As of: July 1, 2025 estimate (Vintage 2025, published May 2026)

Two counties

Draper sits in two counties. The Census Bureau’s July 1, 2025 estimates put 47,159 residents in the Salt Lake County part of Draper and 3,493 in the Utah County part.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, Utah places and counties file · As of: July 1, 2025 (Vintage 2025)

Silicon Slopes businesses

Draper City says it is part of the Silicon Slopes tech corridor, with more than 1,000 businesses in various industries operating in the city, and names Dealertrack, 1-800 Contacts, Trader Joe’s, Tesla and Minky Couture among them.

Source: Draper City Economic Development · As of: page undated; read October 21, 2026

Transit and airports

Draper City says public transit runs to and from both valleys through UTA’s FrontRunner and TRAX stops in Draper, and that Salt Lake International Airport and Provo Municipal Airport are both only 30 minutes away by car.

Source: Draper City Economic Development · As of: page undated; read October 21, 2026

The Point

The Point is the 600 acres of state-owned property at the old site of the Utah State Prison in Draper. The Point of the Mountain State Land Authority, created by the Utah State Legislature on May 8, 2018, plans and manages its development. The Point’s site says its Promenade will include over 2 million square feet of office space, approximately 3,000 multi-family housing units, and 222,000 square feet of retail, shopping and restaurants, and grocery stores.

Source: The Point of the Mountain State Land Authority, media release · As of: November 29, 2022

Source: The Point (thepointutah.org) · As of: read October 21, 2026

Draper Commercial Real Estate FAQ

What is The Point in Draper?

The 600 acres of state-owned property at the old site of the Utah State Prison in Draper, planned and managed by the Point of the Mountain State Land Authority. Its Promenade is planned to include over 2 million square feet of office space and 222,000 square feet of retail, shopping and restaurants, and grocery stores. (Source: The Point of the Mountain State Land Authority, as of November 29, 2022; Source: The Point, as of October 21, 2026)

Is Draper in Salt Lake County or Utah County?

Both. The Census Bureau’s July 1, 2025 estimates put 47,159 Draper residents in Salt Lake County and 3,493 in Utah County, for 50,652 in all. (Source: U.S. Census Bureau, Utah places and counties file, as of July 1, 2025 (Vintage 2025))

Does Draper have commuter rail?

Yes. Draper City says UTA’s FrontRunner and TRAX both stop in Draper. (Source: Draper City Economic Development, as of October 21, 2026 (page undated))

How do I check zoning in Draper?

Find the property on the Zoning Map linked from Draper’s Planning and Development page, then read that zone in the City Code linked from the same page. Confirm with the Planning Division before you commit. (Source: Draper Planning and Development, as of October 21, 2026)

How many businesses are in Draper?

More than 1,000 businesses in various industries, according to Draper City. (Source: Draper City Economic Development, as of October 21, 2026 (page undated))
