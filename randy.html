About Randy

Randy Cummins spent more than 30 years in marketing and advertising, helping create campaigns for national brands such as Intel, Coca-Cola, ICON Fitness, Nature's Way and Hilton Hotels, as well as for Utah developers and home builders including Fieldstone Homes, BIG Homes and Hubble Homes.

In early 2009, during the market crash, Randy started Veyron Financial, Inc. Working with local law firms, the company helped homeowners avoid losing their homes to lender foreclosure.

After a successful career in mortgage resolution, Randy joined REMAX Associates as a licensed real estate agent. A REALTOR® on Team Utah Commercial, he specializes in land acquisition and commercial development, along with commercial sales and leasing. He earned REMAX Utah's 100% Club award in 2022 and its Executive Club award in 2023 and 2024.

Outside of work, Randy restores and collects classic cars, and still sits in now and then with a vintage rock band.
