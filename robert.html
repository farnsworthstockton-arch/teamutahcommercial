About Robert

A lifetime resident of Salt Lake City, Utah, Robert Farnsworth has dedicated his career to serving others. In 1984 Robert began selling real estate, and by 1990 he had completed the broker examination and licensing requirements set by the Utah Division of Real Estate.

Robert holds an active Utah Real Estate Associate Broker License, having met the Division's requirements for advanced broker education, examination and qualifying experience. Over the years he continued his education by earning his CSP, CRS and GRI designations, and he is a REALTOR® with the National Association of REALTORS®.

Recognized in the real estate industry as a leader, Robert has served on the Board of Directors for the local, state and national REALTOR® Associations, and on the Wasatch Front Regional Multiple Listing Service Board of Directors.

Robert has been with REMAX Associates since 2005 and recently celebrated 40 years in the business, earning REALTOR® Emeritus status from the National Association of REALTORS®. Today he leads Team Utah Commercial, which RE/MAX ranked the #1 team in Utah by commercial commissions in both 2024 and 2025, and he hosts the team's free Commercial Mentoring classes for agents, owners and investors.

His team's work was featured in REALTOR® Magazine in 2025 in an article titled "Outperforming Goliaths."
