Washington City Commercial Real Estate Basics

Population

37,107

residents, the U.S. Census Bureau estimate for Washington city on July 1, 2025. The April 1, 2020 estimates base was 28,000.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, City and Town Population Totals: 2020-2025 · As of: July 1, 2025 estimate (Vintage 2025, published May 2026)

Its own city, in a fast-growing area

The Census Bureau publishes its own population estimate for Washington city, in Washington County. Washington City Economic Development says the city is in the southwest corner of the state, at the center of one of the most rapidly growing areas in Utah.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, Utah places and counties file · As of: Vintage 2025

Source: Washington City Economic Development · As of: read October 21, 2026

Three Interstate 15 exits

The city’s 2022 Retail Profile says Washington City has 3 exits off Interstate-15 and calls Exit 13 the gateway to Washington County. Its map shows Exit 10, with existing stores including Walmart Supercenter, The Home Depot and Kohl’s; Exit 11, a new Washington Main Street exit that was then planned to open early 2024; and Exit 13.

Source: Washington City 2022 Retail Profile (EDCUtah, prepared for Washington City) · As of: 2022 edition; read October 21, 2026

Trade area

The same 2022 profile, using Esri 2021 estimates, counts 116,789 people within a 15-minute drive and 171,020 within a 30-minute drive, and a median household income of $68,264 in the city itself.

Source: Washington City 2022 Retail Profile (EDCUtah, prepared for Washington City) · As of: Esri 2021 estimates, published 2022

Projects the city is promoting

Washington City Economic Development features three projects: the Interstate 15 Buena Vista Commercial Corridor, the ARA Southwest Logistics Center and the Sunrise Valley Industrial Park.

Source: Washington City Economic Development · As of: read October 21, 2026

Zoning & Land Use in Washington City

Washington City publishes its city code online (linked from the city’s Records page). Its GIS and Maps page has an interactive Zoning Districts map, a printable zoning map and the General Plan map. The Planning & Zoning page says all applications and plans are submitted, reviewed and permitted online through City Inspect.

Washington City Commercial Real Estate FAQ

Is Washington City the same as St. George?

No. Washington City is its own city, and the Census Bureau estimates its population separately: 37,107 residents on July 1, 2025, up from an April 1, 2020 estimates base of 28,000. (Source: U.S. Census Bureau, City and Town Population Totals: 2020-2025, as of July 1, 2025 (Vintage 2025); Source: Washington City Economic Development, as of October 21, 2026)

How many Interstate 15 exits serve Washington City?

Three, according to the city’s 2022 Retail Profile, which shows Exits 10, 11 and 13 and calls Exit 13 the gateway to Washington County. (Source: Washington City 2022 Retail Profile, as of 2022 edition)

What commercial projects is Washington City promoting?

Its economic development page features the Interstate 15 Buena Vista Commercial Corridor, the ARA Southwest Logistics Center and the Sunrise Valley Industrial Park. (Source: Washington City Economic Development, as of October 21, 2026)

How do I check zoning in Washington City?

Find the property on the city’s interactive Zoning Districts map, then read that zone in the online city code linked from the city’s Records page. Confirm with Planning & Zoning before you commit. (Source: Washington City GIS and Maps, as of October 21, 2026)

How many people live within a 30-minute drive of Washington City?

The city’s 2022 Retail Profile, using Esri 2021 estimates, counts 171,020 people within a 30-minute drive and 116,789 within a 15-minute drive. (Source: Washington City 2022 Retail Profile, as of Esri 2021 estimates)
