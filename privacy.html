Privacy Policy

What happens to the information you send us through this website.

Effective November 19, 2026

This website belongs to Team Utah Commercial, a commercial real estate team brokered by REMAX Associates, 6955 S Union Park Center, Suite 140, Cottonwood Heights, UT 84047. This policy covers teamutahcommercial.com. In short: we only collect what you type into our forms, we use it to answer you, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • What you type into a form. For example your name, email address, phone number, company, details about a property or the space you need, your message, and whether you ticked the “OK to call or text me” box.
  • A few details sent with every form, used only to stop spam and abuse: the page you sent it from, the date and time, your internet (IP) address and your browser type.

The calculators and tools on this site run in your own browser. Numbers you type into them are not sent to us unless you put them in a form and send it. Some tools, like the Due Diligence Checklist, remember your progress in your own browser only; we cannot see it.

How we use it

  • To answer your request and follow up with you about it.
  • To send you market updates, only if you asked for them.
  • To keep spam and abuse off our forms.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it for anyone else’s marketing.

Who else sees it

  • The agents on our team, and REMAX Associates, when needed to help you.
  • The service providers that carry it for us: a hosting company runs the private servers that receive our forms and alert our team (by email, and by a short notification on a team phone), and Google (Gmail) delivers each request to our team by email. They handle it only to provide that service.
  • Anyone the law requires us to give it to, such as under a court order.

Like most websites, some pages load pieces from other companies, which see your IP address when your browser asks for them: icons and fonts from Cloudflare’s cdnjs and jsDelivr, map tiles from OpenStreetMap, and a Google map on the consultation page. Their own privacy policies apply. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics tools, and our forms do not set cookies.

Calls and texts

Some forms have an “OK to call or text me” box. It starts unticked and is optional: you never have to tick it to get help. If you tick it, you agree that our team may call or text you about your request at the number you gave. Message and data rates may apply. You can tell us to stop at any time by replying STOP to a text, or by telling us any other way. Ticking it only covers contact about your request; it is not permission to send you marketing calls or texts.

Email updates

If you ask for market updates, you can stop them at any time by replying “unsubscribe” or emailing us. Every update we send will tell you how.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to help you and to keep the business records that real estate brokerages must keep. After that we delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner, and we will, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email Robert@teamutahcre.com or call 801-898-8810.

Children

This website is for adults doing business in commercial real estate. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Forms are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and only our team can open the requests. No method of sending or storing information is perfectly secure, so please don’t send things like bank account numbers or passwords through a website form.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
