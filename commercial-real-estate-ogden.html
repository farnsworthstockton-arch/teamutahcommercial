Featured development sites

  • Business Depot Ogden (BDO): a 1,000+ acre master-planned business park with over 9 million square feet of existing space and in excess of 300 acres for new development.
  • Ogden Business Exchange (OBE): available lots range from 2.5 to 5 AC, with building footprints from 14,000 to 90,000 SF.
  • Kemp Ogden Gateway Center: a 47-acre business airpark with 1.3 million square feet of buildable space.
  • Ogden-Hinckley Airport (OGD): within two miles of Hill Air Force Base and less than 10 minutes from downtown Ogden.

Source: Ogden City Economic Development, Featured Development Sites · As of: page undated; read October 21, 2026

Target industries

Ogden City Economic Development targets five industries: Aerospace & Defense, Outdoor Products, Life Sciences, Software & Technology, and Advanced Materials & Manufacturing. The same page places Hill Air Force Base two miles from Ogden.

Source: Ogden City Economic Development, Target Industries · As of: page undated; read October 21, 2026

Zoning & Land Use in Ogden

Ogden City’s Planning page links to the city’s Zoning Ordinance and an online Zoning Map.

  • Ogden City Planning: the city’s planning page
  • Zoning Ordinance (Ogden City Code): the code link the city publishes
  • Ogden Zoning Map: look up the zone for a property

Source: Ogden City Planning · As of: read October 21, 2026

Ogden is rewriting its zoning. Through the Zone Ogden project the city is writing a Unified Development Code that would fully replace Titles 14, 15, 17 and 18 of the city code. The city says the complete draft was finished in February 2026, the Planning Commission has recommended approval, and the next step is City Council review. Check which code applies before you rely on a zoning answer.

Source: Ogden City, Zone Ogden (Development Code rewrite) · As of: status as posted on October 21, 2026

Ogden Commercial Real Estate FAQ

Who are the largest employers in Ogden?

Ogden City lists the Internal Revenue Service at 7,000 - 9,999 employees, McKay Dee Hospital Center at 3,000 - 3,999 and America First Credit Union at 2,000 - 2,999, followed by Autoliv, Weber State University, Fresenius USA and Ogden Regional Medical Center at 1,000 - 1,999 each. (Source: Ogden City, Largest Employers, as of March 2026 (Utah Firm Find))

Is Ogden changing its zoning code?

Yes. The Zone Ogden project is a Unified Development Code that would fully replace Titles 14, 15, 17 and 18. The city says the draft was finished in February 2026, the Planning Commission has recommended approval, and City Council review is next. (Source: Ogden City, Zone Ogden, as of October 21, 2026)

Where are the big business parks in Ogden?

Ogden City Economic Development features Business Depot Ogden, a 1,000+ acre master-planned business park; the Ogden Business Exchange, with lots from 2.5 to 5 AC; the Kemp Ogden Gateway Center, a 47-acre business airpark; and Ogden-Hinckley Airport. (Source: Ogden City Economic Development, Featured Development Sites, as of October 21, 2026 (page undated))

How do I look up the zoning for an Ogden property?

Use the Zoning Map linked from Ogden City’s Planning page, then read that zone in the Zoning Ordinance linked from the same page. Confirm with the city, especially while Zone Ogden is under review. (Source: Ogden City Planning, as of October 21, 2026)

How many people live in Ogden?

89,510, the U.S. Census Bureau estimate for July 1, 2025. Ogden is in Weber County. (Source: U.S. Census Bureau, City and Town Population Totals: 2020-2025, as of July 1, 2025 (Vintage 2025); Source: U.S. Census Bureau, Utah places and counties file, as of July 1, 2025 (Vintage 2025))
