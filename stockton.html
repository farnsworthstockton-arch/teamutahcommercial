About Stockton

Stockton Farnsworth has been a licensed Utah real estate professional since July 2015. After completing the advanced broker curriculum and qualifying experience required by the Utah Division of Real Estate, Stockton upgraded his license to an active Utah Real Estate Associate Broker License, earned by completing 120 hours of approved broker education at a certified Utah pre-licensing school, passing the broker exam, and documenting three years of full-time licensed experience and at least 60 experience points.

As an Associate Broker with Team Utah Commercial at REMAX Associates, Stockton works on industrial, retail, office, land, multi-family and hospitality transactions across Utah. He is a candidate pursuing the CCIM Designation.

Committed to continuous education, Stockton pursues advanced certifications across negotiation, listing strategy, business development, commercial sales and leasing, marketing and emerging technology. He has served on the Salt Lake Board of REALTORS® Government Affairs Committee and the Utah Association of REALTORS® Legislative Committee, and teaches in the team's free Commercial Mentoring classes.

Full disclosure: Stockton grew up as a Boy Scout with a soft spot for collecting merit badges. As a REALTOR®, professional designations turned out to be the closest grown-up equivalent, which goes a long way toward explaining the credential wall below.
