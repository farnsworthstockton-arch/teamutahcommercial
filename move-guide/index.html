My math: a 30-year fixed loan at 7.4%, Freddie Mac's average on Oct 8, 20263. Rates change every week. A year earlier (Oct 9, 2025) the average was 6.3%, and the same 20%-down loan would have been about $2,595 a month.

Property tax: Utah only taxes 55% of a main home's value

Utah takes 45% off the value of your primary residence before it figures the tax8. Example, using Lehi's main tax area (final 2025 rate 0.8373%)9:

$524,000 × 55% × 0.8373% = about $2,413 a year, or $201 a month.

Each town's rate is different, and rates can differ inside a town. See page 3.

Put it together (20% down, in Lehi)

$2,902 principal and interest + $201 property tax = about $3,103 a month

That's before homeowners insurance, HOA dues (if the home has them) and mortgage insurance (if your loan needs it). Those vary too much to guess, so get quotes.

Schools · Roads and railUtah County Move Guide

3The school district change (facts only)

Alpine School District runs its schools through June 30, 2027. On July 1, 2027, three new districts take over17:

  • Aspen Peaks: Alpine, American Fork, Cedar Hills, Highland and Lehi, plus the Utah County part of Draper.
  • Lake Mountain: Cedar Fort, Fairfield, Eagle Mountain and Saratoga Springs, plus unincorporated Utah County west of Lehi.
  • Timpanogos: Lindon, Orem, Pleasant Grove and Vineyard.

Some school boundaries may change where they cross the new district lines17. Herriman and Riverton are in Jordan School District, which isn't part of this split18.

I don't rate or rank schools. To check any address, use the district's own website and the Utah School Report Card at reportcard.schools.utah.gov20.

4Roads and rail

  • Mountain View Corridor: a 4-mile freeway stretch opened in December 2025, between 2100 North in Lehi and Porter Rockwell Boulevard21.
  • 2100 North freeway, Lehi: a 2.8-mile freeway link between I-15 and the Mountain View Corridor ($621 million), under construction through late 2028. UDOT estimates it will save about 12 minutes each way at rush hour21.
  • Pioneer Crossing: new lanes opened May 4, 2026, adding 50% more capacity on the 4.5 miles between Lehi and Saratoga Springs. Work continues through late 202622.
  • FrontRunner 2X: 26.1 miles of double track, for trains every 15 minutes at rush hour and every 30 minutes all day. Construction is planned for 2027 through 2030, depending on a federal grant23.

Your first 60 days · Building newUtah County Move Guide

5Your first 60 days

  • Vehicles: title and register them in Utah within 60 days24.
  • Emissions test: Utah County and Salt Lake County both require one for vehicles that qualify. Check whether yours needs one before you register25.
  • Driver license: with a valid out-of-state license, you'll take a 25-question open-book knowledge test, and a driving test may also be required. Bring proof of identity, your Social Security number, proof of your Utah address and your current license26.
  • Keep your 45% tax break: if the county assessor mails you a residential property declaration (form PT-19A), sign it and return it within 90 days8.
  • Schools: look up your exact address on the district's own website. Boundaries may shift when the new districts start in 202717.
  • Utilities: call your city to start water, sewer and garbage, and ask which power and natural gas companies serve your address.

6Buying a new build?

  • Ask the builder how they work with buyer's agents, before your first visit.
  • Read the builder's contract with your agent before you sign.
  • Get your own inspections.
  • Get the HOA documents and fees in writing.
  • Get the list of what's included vs. what's an upgrade, and the build timeline, in writing.
  • Ask for the property tax rate on that exact lot. Special districts can raise it (see page 3).

First-time buyer help on new homes

Utah Housing Corporation's program lets first-time buyers borrow up to $20,000 toward a newly built home no one has lived in, priced at $450,000 or less. You must qualify for a Utah Housing mortgage through a participating lender. Funds last until they run out, so ask a lender whether money is left27.
