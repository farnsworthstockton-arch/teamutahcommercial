Questions

The ones we get asked most.

Do I have to be a licensed agent?
No. The classes are built for agents, but owners and investors are welcome and get a lot out of them — especially Underwriting the Deal and Marketing the Deal.
Do I have to take them in order?
No — that's the whole point of the redesign. Each class is self-contained. Start wherever you want and pick up the rest as they come around.
Is there a cost?
None. The classes are free and there's nothing sold at the end.
Do I have to switch brokerages to attend?
Absolutely not. Agents from any office are welcome. Come learn the system and take it back to wherever you hang your license.
Can I bring one of my own listings?
Please do. Bring a listing that's gone quiet and we'll pull it up and work on it with the room — that's usually the most useful part of the hour.
