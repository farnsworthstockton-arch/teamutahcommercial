Your Comparison

Rent is figured month by month. Free rent months come first, at the start of the lease. Rent goes up every 12 months from the start of the lease, including during free rent. The TI allowance is counted as money you get at the start. Totals are rounded to the nearest dollar; rents per square foot to the nearest cent.

How the Math Works

Every result on this page comes only from the numbers you type in. The page uses no outside data, market rents or rules.

  • Monthly rent = starting rent per square foot × square feet ÷ 12 for months 1 to 12. In each new 12-month period it goes up by the rent increase percent.
  • Total rent paid = every month’s rent over the term, added up, minus the rent for the free months at the start.
  • TI allowance = TI allowance per square foot × square feet.
  • Average rent paid per square foot per year = total rent paid ÷ square feet ÷ years in the term (months ÷ 12). Free rent is counted; the TI allowance is not.
  • Net effective rent per square foot per year (simple) = (total rent paid − TI allowance) ÷ square feet ÷ years in the term.
  • Net effective rent per square foot per year (discounted), only when you enter a discount rate: each month’s rent paid is discounted back to the start of the lease at the discount rate ÷ 12 per month (rent is paid at the start of each month), and the TI allowance is subtracted at the start. That present value is then spread into one level monthly amount over the term with the same present value, × 12 ÷ square feet. At a 0% discount rate it equals the simple figure.
  • Which offer is lower: for a tenant, a lower net effective rent means the offer costs less per square foot per year once free rent and the TI allowance are counted.

What This Calculator Leaves Out

Net effective rent compares base rent and concessions. Other lease terms can matter just as much.

  • Operating costs. NNN charges, common area costs, utilities and who pays for repairs are not included. Compare them separately, especially between a NNN offer and a gross offer.
  • Your build-out cost. If your improvements cost more than the TI allowance, you pay the difference. That is not counted here.
  • Other terms, such as renewal options, rights to expand or leave early, a security deposit, personal guarantees and moving costs.
  • Taxes. No tax effects are included.
