Tooele Commercial Real Estate Basics

County seat, with interstate and rail access

Tooele City says it is the largest city in Tooele County and is the county seat, and its Demographics page places the city 35 miles west of Salt Lake International Airport.

Tooele County Economic Development lists Interstate 80, State Route 36 (“connecting directly to I-80 and Salt Lake City”) and Union Pacific Rail (“direct freight access to national and international markets”) among the county’s connections.

Source: Tooele City, Demographics (Tooele City Profile) · As of: page undated (©Tooele City 2026); read October 21, 2026
Source: Tooele County Economic Development, Location Advantages · As of: page undated (© 2026); read October 21, 2026

The Peterson Industrial Depot

Tooele City’s Economic Development Strategic Plan lists the Peterson/Ninigret Industrial Depot among the city’s strengths: infrastructure and utilities in place, rail access, and a regional job center. The plan also calls it the city’s major employment center and lists easy access to I-80 as a strength.

The city’s General Plan (Table 20, Annexed Areas & Development) lists the Peterson Industrial Depot PUD at 273.63 total acres, with 267.56 acres used (97.8%) and 6.07 acres vacant (2.2%).

Source: Tooele City Economic Development Strategic Plan · As of: November 2021 (pages 12 and 21); read October 21, 2026
Source: Tooele City General Plan (Revised 2025) · As of: Table 20, Affordable Housing Plan Element adopted December 16, 2020 (page 4.20); read October 21, 2026

Recent industrial projects

Tooele City Economic Development has posted these industrial projects:

  • Central States Manufacturing broke ground at the Peterson Industrial Depot on November 2, 2023.
  • Leitner-Poma broke ground at 408 South Tooele Boulevard on October 16, 2023 and held its grand opening on June 21, 2024. The city says the campus will serve as the headquarters and manufacturing base for its subsidiary, Skytrac.
  • Minova held its groundbreaking at the Peterson Industrial Depot on May 13, 2024.
  • Volusol is moving its headquarters from Salt Lake City to a new building in the Peterson Industrial Depot and anticipates employing 25 to 40 people over the next 2-3 years, in roles from production to rail operations.

Source: Tooele City Economic Development, Projects & News · As of: entries updated December 14, 2023 to August 21, 2024; read October 21, 2026

Zoning & Land Use in Tooele

Tooele City’s Planning Division says its staff help maintain the zoning and development codes, the Zoning and Future Land Use maps and the General Plan, and that land use and subdivision applications are made through the city’s Planning and Zoning Portal, linked from that page. Zoning rules are in Title 7 of the City Code, the Uniform Zoning Title of Tooele City.

  • Tooele City Planning Division: the city’s planning page
  • Maps of Tooele City: accept the city’s disclaimer to open its map viewer, which includes the “Tooele City: Zoning and Land Use Map”
  • Tooele City Code (Title 7, Uniform Zoning Title): the city says the online code may differ from the adopted code, and posts a full code PDF marked current as of October 9, 2026
  • Title 7 Chapter 16 (PDF): purpose and intent of the mixed use, commercial, industrial and special purpose districts
  • Title 7 Chapter 16b (PDF): the Tooele City Business Park zoning district
  • General Plan (Revised 2025, PDF): linked from the city’s Resources and Master Plans page

Source: Tooele City Planning Division · As of: read October 21, 2026
Source: Maps of Tooele City · As of: read October 21, 2026
Source: Tooele City Code · As of: online code read October 21, 2026

Tooele’s industrial zones. Chapter 16 of the zoning title describes the purpose of each industrial district:

  • Light Industrial (LI): locations for light industrial assembly and manufacturing uses that produce no appreciable negative impact to adjacent properties.
  • Industrial Service (IS): locations for more intensive service type uses for the benefit of the community and regional area.
  • Industrial (I): recognizes existing industrial sites and uses and allows additional industrial uses that add to employment opportunities and economic diversity.
  • Research and Development (RD): combines certain elements of the General Commercial and Light Industrial districts to allow a professional business park with a mix of certain small commercial and light industrial uses. The code says it is not intended for large commercial or industrial uses or for intensive retail or manufacturing activities.

Source: Tooele City Code, Title 7 Chapter 16 · As of: Section 7-16-2 as posted in the online code; read October 21, 2026

A rail-served business park zone. Chapter 16b sets up the Tooele City Business Park (TCBP) zoning district for approximately 324 acres of contiguous land adjacent to recorded phases of the Tooele City Commercial Park. Its stated purposes include creating a rail-served mixed-use commercial-industrial business park and fostering renewable energy sources. It is split into Section A (general and neighborhood commercial uses), Section B (lighter-industrial uses) and Section C (heavier-industrial uses).

Source: Tooele City Code, Title 7 Chapter 16b · As of: purposes section Ord. 2023-28 (06-07-2023), use-area section amended by Ord. 2025-28 (10-15-2025), chapter PDF dated Jun2026; read October 21, 2026

New commercial design standards in 2026. Chapter 11c, adopted by Ord. 2026-25 on 09-02-2026, sets design standards for new commercial buildings and qualifying renovations whose facades are next to a public right-of-way, public space, residential dwellings or a future residential area, such as roofline height variations at every 50 feet. Commercial buildings in the historic Downtown Overlay zone and the Industrial zone are exempt. Ask the city whether it applies to your site.

Source: Tooele City Code, Title 7 Chapter 11c · As of: Ord. 2026-25 (09-02-2026); read October 21, 2026

Paying for new infrastructure. Tooele City’s economic development office says Public Infrastructure Districts, first allowed through a 2019 bill passed by the Utah Legislature, can finance new infrastructure for public use, such as roads, water and wastewater systems, utilities, public transportation or parks, with bonds repayable from property taxes or assessments on the property within the district. The city posts its policy (amended November 2024) and application on the same page.

Source: Tooele City, Public Infrastructure Districts · As of: policy amended November 2024; read October 21, 2026

Tooele Commercial Real Estate FAQ

How many people live in Tooele?

42,270, the U.S. Census Bureau estimate for July 1, 2025, up from an April 1, 2020 estimates base of 35,745. Tooele City says it is the largest city in Tooele County and is the county seat. (Source: U.S. Census Bureau, City and Town Population Totals: 2020-2025, as of July 1, 2025 (Vintage 2025); Source: Tooele City, Demographics, as of October 21, 2026)

Where is Tooele’s main industrial area?

The Peterson Industrial Depot. Tooele City’s 2021 Economic Development Strategic Plan calls the Peterson/Ninigret Industrial Depot the city’s major employment center and lists it as a strength, with infrastructure and utilities in place, rail access, and a regional job center. The city has posted groundbreakings there by Central States Manufacturing (November 2, 2023) and Minova (May 13, 2024). (Source: Tooele City Economic Development Strategic Plan, as of November 2021; Source: Tooele City, Projects & News, as of October 21, 2026)

Which companies have announced expansions in Tooele?

Tooele City’s Community Profile lists Leitner-Poma (236 jobs / $35,000,000 capital investment), Central States Manufacturing (150 / $30,000,000), Mountain West Vinyl (65 / 20,000,000, as printed) and Minova (60 / $22,000,000). (Source: Tooele City Community Profile (EDCUtah), as of 2026 edition)

What does Tooele’s RD (Research and Development) zone allow?

Tooele City Code says the RD district combines certain elements of the General Commercial and Light Industrial districts to allow a professional business park with a mix of certain small commercial and light industrial uses. It is not intended for large commercial or industrial uses or for intensive retail or manufacturing activities. Read the full chapter and confirm uses with the Planning Division. (Source: Tooele City Code, Title 7 Chapter 16, as of October 21, 2026)

How do I check the zoning for a Tooele property?

Open the Maps of Tooele City page, accept the city’s disclaimer and use the Tooele City: Zoning and Land Use Map, then read that zone in Title 7 of the City Code. The city says the online code may differ from the adopted code, so confirm with the Planning Division before you commit. (Source: Maps of Tooele City, as of October 21, 2026; Source: Tooele City Code, as of October 21, 2026)
