Where These Numbers Come From

  1. Commercial property value and value change: Utah State Tax Commission, Property Tax Division, 2025 Annual Statistical Report, Table 1A, “Commercial & Industrial” column. The 2024 figures come from the 2024 Annual Statistical Report, same table.
  2. Population: U.S. Census Bureau, county population estimates, Vintage 2025, as of July 1, 2025.
  3. Job change: Utah Department of Workforce Services, nonfarm jobs by county, released October 16, 2026. September 2026 estimates compared with September 2025.
  4. Largest employers: Utah Department of Workforce Services, 2025 Major Employers. The state reports employer size in ranges, so job counts are shown as ranges.

About “commercial property value”: it is the taxable value of locally assessed commercial and industrial land and buildings. Utah taxes commercial property at its full market value as estimated by each county assessor. It includes hotels and resorts, which is part of why Summit County ranks high.

About the value change: it includes new buildings added to the tax rolls, not just price changes, so it is not a sale-price index.

Why these 9 counties: they are the 9 Utah counties with the most commercial and industrial property value in 2025, together holding about 94% of the statewide total.
